Hidden Story

One person holds a secret. Everyone else asks yes/no questions. An incorruptible narrator answers from the hidden story only, and cannot be tricked into telling.

Write your own

No accounts. Takes 30 seconds. Share the room link with friends anywhere.

Write a mystery

Players see the title and premise. The hidden story and the secret stay on the server; they are never sent to anyone's browser until the case is solved.

Tip: mention a few things that did not happen ("nobody was hurt", "it was not a crime"). The narrator answers only from this text.

You will land in your new room with the player link ready to share.

How a round goes

  1. The host writes a mystery, or opens the sample, and shares the room link.
  2. Detectives pick a name and ask yes/no questions: "Was anyone hurt?"
  3. The narrator stamps each one Yes, No, or Not in the story. Attempts to trick it get refused.
  4. Solve it by writing your explanation. The first correct guess wins and the whole story is revealed.
